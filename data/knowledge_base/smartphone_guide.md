Battery capacity is commonly expressed in mAh. A larger capacity can contribute to longer battery life, but actual endurance also depends on display size, brightness, chipset efficiency, software, network use, and workload.

Refresh rate such as 60Hz, 90Hz, or 120Hz describes how often the display can refresh. Higher refresh rates can make motion appear smoother, but may affect power consumption.

128GB, 256GB, and 512GB describe storage capacity. Actual available space is lower than advertised because the operating system and preinstalled software occupy storage.

RAM affects how many applications and processes can remain active. Higher RAM does not automatically mean a phone is faster in every task.

Camera quality cannot be determined from megapixels alone. Sensor size, image processing, stabilization, lenses, and software can all affect results.

Buying principle: compare the specifications that matter for the user's actual use case rather than optimizing for a single number.
